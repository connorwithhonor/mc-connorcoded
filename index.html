The Connor Operating Principles

Read this when you feel stuck. Read it when you feel grandiose. Read it before you spend 4 hours on the wrong thing.

◆ Ship Before Perfect

v1 in public beats v3 in your head. Every additional polish hour past the minimum viable launch is a tax on your real income. Launch ugly, fix in flight, the market tells you what matters.

◆ First Principles, Always

For every tile, ask: what's the root truth? Who pays? How does the dollar move? What unfair advantage am I using? Strip away the marketing language and the AI hype until you hit physics. Then build from there. The strongest entrepreneurs reason from physics, not from analogy.

◆ Recurring Beats One-Time

$497/mo × 100 customers = $49,700/mo. Forever. One $17K listing fee = a great month, then zero. Build recurring revenue first. One-time revenue funds the build of recurring revenue. Never the other way around.

◆ Async or It Doesn't Scale

You're one man. If a build needs you on a phone call, in a meeting, or babysitting a process, it can't scale. Every system must be: forms, video, voice agents, or self-serve. If a recommendation requires a second human, flag it and propose the solo-path version.

◆ Name the Lane

Every move you make should serve one of three things: Revenue, Authority Content, or Automation Systems. If a task doesn't fit any of those three, it's a distraction. Don't let yourself drift.

◆ Compliance Is a Feature

The agent who blasts 10,000 cold texts gets sued. The agent with TCPA-compliant systems sleeps at night. Compliance is not a constraint — it's a moat. Sell it as one.

◆ Receipts > Opinions

Every claim, every pitch, every page: lead with numbers. "$36K vs $17K" beats "save big." "$2.5M off one text" beats "high results." If you can't show the receipt, find one before you ship.

◆ The Two Axes: Money & Longevity

Every answer should move at least one axis. The best ones move both. If a project doesn't either make more money OR keep you operating for the next 30 years, deprioritize it.

◆ One-Man Discipline

Your edge is operating speed and ownership stack — not headcount, not capital. Every system you build should reinforce that edge. Solo + AI-leveraged + recurring revenue = compounding without staff.

◆ Connection Is the Multiplier

RE drives local authority. Authority drives SaaS. SaaS funds RE. Recovery becomes paid product. Every lane feeds another. Never build a project that doesn't connect to at least one other lane.
